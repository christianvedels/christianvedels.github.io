**Status:** Under review at *Explorations in Economic History* · 204+ HuggingFace downloads/month

Linking individuals across historical censuses and tax records requires standardized occupational categories. The standard scheme — HISCO — is assigned manually by experts, making large-scale application prohibitively slow. OccCANINE automates this.

Trained on **14 million labelled records in 13 languages**, OccCANINE is a finetuned CANINE language model that achieves accuracy, recall, and precision above 90% on out-of-sample data. It handles spelling variation, abbreviation, and the archaic vocabulary of historical sources.

OccCANINE has been adopted by researchers across economics, economic history, and historical demography. It is rapidly becoming a field standard for occupational standardization.
