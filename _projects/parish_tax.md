**Status:** Active — data ready, paper at early stage

Parish tax records from 19th-century Denmark encode income information that has never been systematically extracted. This paper uses OccCANINE and CHAOS to convert these records into income estimates, producing — among other results — the **first ever Gini coefficient for Copenhagen**.

The paper contributes to questions about inequality, regional development, and the long-run effects of industrialization on income distributions. It also demonstrates the value of the measurement infrastructure built through OccCANINE and CHAOS: questions that were previously unanswerable become tractable once the tools are in place.
