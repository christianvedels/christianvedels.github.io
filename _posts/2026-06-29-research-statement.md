What drives all of my research is a simple premise: much of what we know about economic development is limited by what we can measure. I build machine learning tools and large-scale historical datasets to recover economic measurements from the past, and combine them with causal inference methods to study how geography and institutions shape prosperity. The broader aim is to use history to expand the set of possibilities economists can make visible to policy makers.

**Keywords:** Economic history, economic development, causal inference, machine learning, economic geography, large-scale historical data.
